Behind the work
rn

raynoesis

A feeling of place.
A practice of detail.

raynoesis is an independent portfolio of Chinese-inspired 3D environments and objects. The work explores how architecture, materials and everyday things can suggest a world beyond the frame.

The creative workflow is AI-assisted, including GPT, alongside procedural construction, material setup and Unity integration. The images shown here are actual Unity renders, without AI repainting.
