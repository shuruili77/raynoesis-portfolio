Before asking

Make the problem
easy to understand.

A few concrete details make technical questions much easier to diagnose. Keep private information and account credentials out of screenshots.

  1. 01

    Name the project & environment

    Include the asset name, package version if available, exact Unity version, URP version, operating system and graphics API.

  2. 02

    Describe the smallest reproduction

    Say which scene or prefab is involved, what you expected and what happened. If possible, check the same case in a clean URP project using the bundled guide.

  3. 03

    Add useful visual evidence

    Include a focused screenshot and relevant Console error text. For material issues, also show the active render pipeline and lighting settings.

Common questions

Good to know.

Where can I buy or download a project?+

The projects are not currently on sale here or listed on the Unity Asset Store. This website is a portfolio and support reference. No store links or download links are available yet.

Which Unity configuration has been tested?+

The current candidates target Unity 6000.6.0f1, URP 17.6.0, Linear color space and Windows Direct3D 11. Other versions, Built-in, HDRP, mobile, VR and WebGL are not verified. Check each project page for its specific limits.

Why are materials pink or the lighting different?+

First confirm that URP is installed and the correct pipeline asset is assigned in Graphics and Quality settings. Follow the bundled setup guide and check the Console for shader errors. Lighting and post-processing also affect appearance; the screenshots show the included demonstration setups.

Do the garden water and warship simulate movement?+

No. Garden water is a static transparent material without simulation or refraction. The warship is a stationary environment asset: it does not include buoyancy, sailing, rowing, rudder animation or cloth simulation.

Can the interiors connect automatically to the estate?+

The exterior estate and four interiors are independent projects. They do not provide an automatically matched floorplan or a seamless building-interior connection.

Are these historical reconstructions?+

They are original artistic interpretations inspired by historical Chinese architecture and objects, rather than measured archaeological reconstructions.

How is AI used in the work?+

The workflow includes GPT assistance and procedural construction, alongside material setup and Unity integration. The portfolio images are actual Unity renders and have not been AI-repainted.
